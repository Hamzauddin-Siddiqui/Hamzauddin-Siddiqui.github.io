Network Structure

  • Stage 1 - Customers: 46 customers placing 9,215 orders
  • Stage 2 - Plants: 19 plants (PLANT01-PLANT19) with varying daily capacities (7-1,070 orders/day)
  • Stage 3 - Origin Ports: 11 ports total:
    • 10 regular origin ports: PORT01, PORT02, PORT03, PORT04, PORT05, PORT06, PORT07, PORT08, PORT10, PORT11
    • 1 dual-role port: PORT09 (serves as BOTH origin AND destination)
    • Note: PORT01 appears in plant-port connections but has no freight rates defined
  • Stage 4 - Carriers: 9 carriers (V444_0, V444_1, V444_2, V444_4, V444_5, V444_6, V444_7, V444_8, V444_9) with service levels (DTD, DTP, CRF)
  • Stage 5 - Destination: Single destination port (PORT09) - same physical location as origin PORT09
  • Temporal Dimension: 9-day planning horizon (May 26 - June 3, 2013)

Key Constraints

  • Plant-port connectivity: Not all plant-port combinations are feasible
  • Product availability: 82.5% of products available at only 1 plant
  • VMI restrictions: 10 customers with restricted plant assignments; 1,045 VMI orders (50.0% of 2,089 VMI orders) are infeasible due to product unavailability at assigned plants
  • Service level requirements: Carrier-service level exclusive pairings
  • Capacity: Daily network capacity = 5,791 orders/day (sum of all plant capacities)
  • Temporal windows: Each order has min/max shipping dates based on delivery date, TPT, and warehousing constraints
